## The problem

Aviation authorities and operators hold equipment — airfield lighting, rescue vehicles, radio — that they could sell, lease or exchange. EQUIP4SAFETY gives them a shared marketplace with verified suppliers.

## The fix

Technical lead of the project and coordination of the development team.

Contributions to the Laravel API and the Next.js front end.

Represented the project within the CCAA’s official delegation at AFI Aviation Week 2025 in Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe.

## The result

Public demo live at demo.equip4safety.org; the project was represented within the CCAA delegation at AFI Aviation Week 2025.
